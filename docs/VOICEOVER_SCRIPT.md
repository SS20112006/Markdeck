# 🎙️ Markdeck Product Launch Video — Voiceover Script

* **Tone**: Confident, direct, conversational builder tone (not a corporate commercial).
* **Pacing**: Steady, deliberate, synced with visuals.
* **Duration**: ~55 seconds of spoken audio.

---

### [00:00 – 00:09] Scene 1: The Frustration & The Solution
> *"As developers and technical speakers, we spend way too much time fighting with slide boxes and formatting in PowerPoint or Keynote, when all we really want to do... is write Markdown. That's why I built Markdeck."*

---

### [00:09 – 00:20] Scene 2: Live Editing & Drag and Drop
> *"On the left, an extensible CodeMirror editor. On the right, a live 16:9 canvas updating in real-time. Have an existing Markdown file? Just drag and drop it straight from your Mac. Zero setup, zero accounts."*

---

### [00:20 – 00:36] Scene 3: Presentation Mode
> *"Hit Command+Enter, and you're in a distraction-free presentation mode. 60 frames-per-second transitions powered by Framer Motion, with full keyboard navigation and automatic aspect ratio preservation."*

---

### [00:36 – 00:46] Scene 4: Interactivity & Celebration
> *"When you reach the finale, hit 'C' to celebrate with confetti directly on stage. Engaging, lightweight, and delightful."*

---

### [00:46 – 00:54] Scene 5: Power Features
> *"Hit Escape to exit, Command+P for pixel-perfect 1-slide-per-page PDF exports, speaker notes support, and local storage auto-persistence."*

---

### [00:54 – 01:00] Scene 6: Outro & Call to Action
> *"Markdeck is built with React 19, Tailwind v4, and Apple Human Interface Guidelines. It's completely free and open source. Try it live today at the link below."*
